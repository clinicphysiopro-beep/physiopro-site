Who We Help

We work with people who move.

PhysioPro works with athletes, active adults, and anyone whose pain or injury is standing between them and the life they want back.

Book your first session ← Back to PhysioPro

Athletes

Recreational and competitive athletes across every sport and training context — from weekend competitors to professionals. The return-to-sport standard is full load capacity and physical confidence, not just clearance to train through discomfort.

Active Adults

People who train, move, and stay active — and want to keep doing that without pain setting the limit. Gym training, hiking, cycling, recreational sport — the rehabilitation goal is the full activity, not a modified version of it.

Runners

IT band syndrome, plantar fasciitis, stress reactions, Achilles tendinopathy, and the full range of running-related load presentations. Assessment includes movement analysis and training load review. The goal is running — not just walking without pain.

Combat Sports Athletes

BJJ, wrestling, boxing, Muay Thai, MMA — high-contact sports with specific injury patterns and demanding return-to-training requirements. Rehabilitation is built around the demands of sparring and competition, not generic sport clearance.

Post-Surgical Patients

ACL reconstruction, rotator cuff repair, hip arthroplasty, spinal surgery — any post-operative case that needs a structured rehabilitation pathway beyond hospital discharge. Milestones are objective, timelines are honest, and the finish line is function.

Desk Workers

Chronic neck tension, thoracic stiffness, and persistent low back pain driven by prolonged static posture. The approach addresses tissue capacity, movement habits, and postural load — not just the symptom at the end of a long day.
