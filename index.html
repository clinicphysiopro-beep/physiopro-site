6 · Before You Book

The questions that matter before you book.

Does the first session include assessment and treatment?

Yes. The first session includes clinical assessment, initial treatment, and a clear next-step plan.

What if I've already been told to rest or stop training?

That may be part of a phase, but it should not automatically be the end of the process. The question here is when and how to return well.

Do you work with chronic pain, sports injuries, and post-op recovery?

Yes. The approach changes with the case, but the direction stays the same: function, capacity, and confidence.

How many sessions will I need?

That depends on your condition, history, and goal. After the first session, we give you an honest estimate.

How does the reservation deposit work?

Some appointment slots may require a deposit to confirm. The deposit is applied to your first session.

Do I need imaging — an X-ray or MRI — before booking?

No. Clinical examination is the primary diagnostic tool. If you have existing imaging, bring it — it helps. But it is not required and waiting for a scan before you start is rarely necessary. Assessment begins from what we find in person, not from a report.

I've tried physio before and it didn't last. Why would this be different?

The most common reason: treatment reduced the pain without rebuilding the capacity. When the load returned, the pain returned. Here, the goal is to restore what the tissue can tolerate — not just to make the pain quiet down temporarily. Assessment identifies the deficit. Treatment builds it back.
