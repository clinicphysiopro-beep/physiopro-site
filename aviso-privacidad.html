Datos recabados

2. Personal Data Collected.

PhysioPro collects the following categories of personal data through this website and associated communication channels:

Identification data
First and last name.
Contact data
WhatsApp phone number, email address (where provided), and general location (city/region, as inferred by Google Analytics).
Service inquiry data
Type of service or condition of interest, as selected or described in contact forms.
Health-related information (sensitive data)
Information voluntarily submitted through the "Ask Leonardo" form or via WhatsApp may include descriptions of physical conditions, symptoms, injuries, surgical history, or treatment history. Finalidades

3. Purposes of Processing.

Primary purposes (required for service):

  • To respond to inquiries submitted via forms or WhatsApp.
  • To schedule and coordinate clinical appointments.
  • To triage whether PhysioPro is an appropriate clinical fit for the prospective patient's condition and goals.
  • To communicate appointment confirmations, reminders, and follow-up information.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

Secondary purposes (optional):

  • To analyze website traffic and user behavior for site improvement purposes (via Google Analytics 4).
  • To send general clinical education or service information where the user has requested it.

Retention. PhysioPro has not established a formal retention period for website leads or WhatsApp conversations. Information is kept for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and manage any resulting care relationship, and you may request cancellation of your data at any time as described in Section 6.

Transferencias

4. Third-Party Transfers.

Personal data may be shared with or processed by the following third parties as part of normal service operations. These transfers occur because the platforms are used to deliver the service — not for commercial sale of your data.

WhatsApp / Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.
When a user submits a contact form or clicks a WhatsApp link, their name and phone number are transferred to WhatsApp (a Meta company) to initiate communication. WhatsApp's privacy policy governs how Meta handles this data. WhatsApp messages may be routed through servers outside of Mexico. WhatsApp Privacy Policy →
Google LLC (Analytics)
Google Analytics 4 collects browsing data through cookies and similar technologies. Google may store and process this data on servers outside of Mexico. Google Privacy Policy →
Cloudflare Pages (Hosting)
This website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process standard web request data such as IP address, user agent, timestamps, and requested URLs for security, delivery, and operational purposes. Cloudflare Privacy Policy →

Form submissions. Data submitted through website forms (name, phone number, stated goal or question, and campaign/landing-page parameters) is verified with Cloudflare Turnstile, routed through Cloudflare infrastructure, and then transferred to PhysioPro's own record system. Cloudflare Turnstile is loaded on the pages that contain these forms.

PhysioPro does not sell, rent, or commercially transfer personal data to any third party beyond the platforms listed above.

Datos sensibles

5. Sensitive Data and Health Information.

Health-related information voluntarily submitted through forms or WhatsApp constitutes datos personales sensibles under LFPDPPP Article 3(VI). This includes descriptions of physical conditions, injuries, symptoms, surgical history, or treatment history.

Pursuant to LFPDPPP Article 8, processing of sensitive personal data requires express, written consent. This consent is requested through the consent checkbox on the "Ask Leonardo" form. By checking the consent box and submitting information, you expressly consent to the processing of health-related information you choose to share for the purposes described in Section 3.

You are not required to provide health-related information to make an inquiry or book an appointment. Detailed clinical history is collected in person at the first session, not through this website.

Rastreo digital

7. Tracking Technologies.

The general pages of this website use Google Analytics 4 (GA4), a web analytics service provided by Google LLC. GA4 uses cookies and similar technologies to collect information about how visitors use the site. GA4 is not loaded on the Exercise Prescription questionnaire and its consultation and information pages (/es/fuerza-y-acondicionamiento/ and /strength-conditioning/). The site does not currently display a cookie-consent banner; GA4 loads when a general page is opened, and the opt-out options below are available.

Google Fonts. The general pages load typefaces from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser sends a request to Google that includes technical data such as your IP address and browser information. The Exercise Prescription pages above serve their fonts from this website and make no Google Fonts request.

Hosting. The website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which processes standard web-request data (IP address, user agent, timestamps, requested URLs) for delivery and security. Cloudflare also serves a small security (bot-detection) script on pages of this website, including the Exercise Prescription pages, as observed on the live site; it is operated by Cloudflare and is not used by PhysioPro for marketing.

Data collected may include: pages visited, time on site, approximate geographic location (city/region level), device type, browser type, and referral source. PhysioPro uses this data for site improvement.

For details on how Google processes analytics data, see the Google Privacy Policy and Google Analytics data practices.

To opt out of Google Analytics tracking, you may install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on or adjust your browser's cookie settings. See the Cookies notice for more information.

For guidance on opting out of tracking technologies in general, consult the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page or your browser's privacy settings.
