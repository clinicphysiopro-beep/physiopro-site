What We Treat

From acute injury to chronic load.

Clinical assessment from session one. Each condition managed with structured progression and a clear return-to-activity timeline.

Knee Pain

ACL reconstruction, meniscus tears, runner's knee, patellofemoral syndrome, and post-surgical loading protocols. Each case is assessed for load tolerance, movement pattern, and readiness to progress. The goal is not pain-free standing — it is full return to whatever loaded activity matters to you.

Shoulder Pain

Rotator cuff pathology, impingement, instability, and labral issues. Assessment is movement-based: what positions load the structure, what relieves it, and what the tissue needs to recover. Progression moves from pain control to full overhead loading and sport-specific demand.

Back Pain

Lumbar disc, facet joint, sacroiliac, and referred leg pain. Most persistent back pain responds to structured loading — not prolonged rest. The approach is graded movement, tissue tolerance, and restoring physical confidence in spinal loading under real-world conditions.

Neck Pain

Cervical disc, facet, and postural load syndromes — including arm referral and tension headaches. Treatment targets the mechanical driver rather than the surface symptom. Loaded neck and thoracic mobility are part of every return-to-full-function plan.

Sports Injuries

Sprains, ligament tears, muscle strains, tendinopathy, and overuse injuries across every sport and training context. Return-to-sport timelines are built on objective load capacity — not arbitrary calendar dates. Full function, not just absence of pain, is the target.

Post-Surgical Rehabilitation

Structured recovery after ACL reconstruction, rotator cuff repair, hip or knee replacement, and other orthopaedic procedures. Rehabilitation begins early and progresses through clear functional milestones. Coordinated with surgical follow-up where required.

Ankle Sprain

Lateral and high ankle sprains, chronic instability, and return-to-running progression built on balance and strength benchmarks — not a fixed timeline.

Tennis & Golfer's Elbow

Progressive tendon loading for lateral and medial elbow tendinopathy, built around your grip strength and racquet or club-sport demands.

Hip Pain

Impingement, labral tears, gluteal and hamstring-origin tendinopathy, and post-surgical hip recovery for athletes and active adults.
