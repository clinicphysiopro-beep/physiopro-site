Exercise prescription · Program information

You don’t need another routine. You need direction.

Discover how a training program works when it takes your experience, goals and progress into account, instead of handing you a generic routine.

See how it works ↓

No deadline and no pressure to decide.

The problem

Why a generic routine falls short.

  • It doesn’t know where you’re starting from or how you move.
  • It repeats the same stimulus even as your body and level change.
  • No one checks your technique or adjusts the load when you need it.
  • Without tracking, it’s hard to know whether you’re really progressing.

The alternative

Individualized exercise prescription.

Generic routine

  • The same for everyone.
  • Copied from the internet or a gym.
  • Doesn’t adjust as you advance.

The PhysioPro program

  • Starts from your experience and goals.
  • Designed and supervised by a physical therapist.
  • Progresses and adjusts with you.

The process

How it works.

  1. 01

    Free consultation

    A conversation of about 30 minutes, in person or virtual, about your goal and your experience. It is not a clinical assessment and carries no obligation to enroll.

  2. 02

    Three technique sessions, in the first week

    In person, at our Zona Río clinic in Tijuana, spread over three days: you define your starting point, work on your technique and learn to run your program.

  3. 03

    Individual program, minimum three calendar months

    Programming built for you, with tracking, reviews of about 30 minutes on a flexible cadence, and adjustments throughout the program.

The program

What’s included.

  • Three initial instruction sessions, in person, during the first week.
  • Individual exercise programming, adjusted to your progress.
  • Progress reviews of about 30 minutes, on a flexible cadence (weekly, biweekly, monthly or another, depending on your case).
  • Tracking and adjustments over a minimum of three calendar months.
  • General education on nutrition, macros, recovery and habits, with no meal plan or medical nutrition therapy.
  • WhatsApp support for program questions.
  • Rescheduling with at least 24 hours’ notice, subject to availability.

Who designs it

Why a physical therapist.

  • Defines your starting point and adapts load progression to your case.
  • Applies technique and biomechanics to every exercise.
  • Supervises your program and adjusts it based on your tracking: technique, progression and training tolerance.
  • Educates you so you build autonomy instead of depending on a trainer.
  • Who it’s for: adults who want to start training strength, return after time away, or stop guessing, and who want a professional plan. It’s hybrid: it needs in-person attendance in Zona Río, Tijuana.
  • Looking for treatment for pain or a current injury? A physical therapy evaluation is the better first step.

Common questions

What people usually ask.

Do I need gym experience?

No. Your starting point is defined individually.

Does the consultation cost anything?

No. The consultation is free (about 30 minutes, in person or virtual) with no obligation to enroll; the program is not free.

Do I have to come to the clinic?

Yes. The three initial sessions are in person at Zona Río, Tijuana. The exact address is shared once your appointment is confirmed.

How often are progress reviews?

They last about 30 minutes and their frequency is individualized to your case (for example weekly, biweekly or monthly).

Can I reschedule appointments?

Yes, with at least 24 hours’ notice and subject to availability.

How long is it and what does it cost?

A minimum of three calendar months. Cost and terms are explained in the consultation, before you decide whether to enroll.

Is progress tracking physical therapy?

No. It exists to track and adjust your training; it does not replace physical therapy.
