The problem

Why a generic routine falls short.

  • It isn't built around your goal or your experience: it's the same for everyone.
  • A generic routine doesn't check your technique or adjust the load as your level changes.
  • Without follow-up, it's hard to know whether you're really progressing.
  • It repeats the same stimulus even as your body and your level change.

The PhysioPro approach

A program built for you.

  • Individualized programming

    Starts from your goal and your experience, and adjusts as you progress.

  • Supervised technique

    Three in-person sessions in your first week, guided by a physical therapist.

  • Follow-up and adjustments

    Progress reviews and adjustments for a minimum of three calendar months.

This is a training and general-education program designed by a physical therapist. It is not physical therapy, and it is not an assessment or treatment of pain or injury.

How it works

Three months, step by step.

  1. 01

    Free consultation

    About 30 minutes, in person or virtual. We talk about your goal and your experience, explain the program and answer your questions.

  2. 02

    First week: three technique sessions

    Three in-person sessions in Zona Río, Tijuana, spread over three days: you set your starting point, work on your technique and learn to carry out your initial programming.

  3. 03

    Individual progression

    A plan built for you, with WhatsApp support and general guidance on nutrition and supplementation (not a meal plan or medical nutrition therapy).

  4. 04

    Reviews and adjustments

    Reviews of about 30 minutes when scheduled, on a flexible cadence (weekly, every two weeks, monthly or otherwise), plus adjustments for a minimum of three calendar months.

The program

What's included.

  • Three initial in-person instruction sessions during the first week.
  • Individual exercise programming, adjusted to your progress.
  • Progress reviews of about 30 minutes when scheduled, on a flexible cadence (weekly, every two weeks, monthly or otherwise, depending on your case).
  • Follow-up and adjustments for a minimum of three calendar months.
  • General guidance on nutrition and supplementation, without a meal plan or medical nutrition therapy.
  • WhatsApp support for program questions.
  • Appointment rescheduling with at least 24 hours' notice, subject to availability.

Who it's for

Is it for you?

It's for you if you…

  • Are an adult who wants to start strength training with structure.
  • Are returning to the gym after a break.
  • Want someone to check your technique and guide your progression.

It's not what you need if you…

  • Are looking for treatment for pain or a current injury: a physical therapy evaluation is a better first step.
  • Can't attend in person at Zona Río: the first three sessions are in person.

Common questions

Quick answers.

Do I need previous gym experience?

No. We set your starting point individually, whether or not you have experience.

What happens during the first week?

Three in-person technique sessions in Zona Río, spread over three days, to set your starting point, work on your technique and learn to carry out your initial programming.

How much time do I need to commit?

There are three in-person sessions in the first week and a minimum commitment of three calendar months. How much time you spend training each week is something we set with you when we build your program, based on your goal and your experience.

How are reviews scheduled?

The cadence is flexible (weekly, every two weeks, monthly or otherwise, depending on your case) and each scheduled review takes about 30 minutes. You can reschedule with at least 24 hours' notice, subject to availability.

What if I have pain or an injury?

This program is not an assessment or a treatment, and it does not replace physical therapy. If you are looking for treatment for pain or a current injury, a physical therapy evaluation is a better first step.

What happens during the free consultation?

A conversation of about 30 minutes, in person or virtual, with no obligation to enroll. We talk about your goal and your experience, explain how the program works and whether it fits you, and answer your questions. If you want to continue, we explain the terms right there.

Does it cost anything?

The consultation is free; the program is not. The cost and terms are explained in the consultation, before you decide.

Do I have to come to the clinic?

Yes. The first three sessions are in person in Zona Río, Tijuana. The consultation can be in person or virtual; the exact address is shared when your appointment is confirmed.
