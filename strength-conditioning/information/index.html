Overview

What the program includes.

  • Initial evaluation in person, to define your starting point.
  • Three technique sessions in the clinic during week one.
  • Individual exercise programming, adjusted weekly.
  • Weekly in-person review of up to 60 minutes during active program weeks.
  • WhatsApp support for program questions.
  • General nutrition and recovery guidance within our professional scope.

Who it’s designed for

Built for people who want structure.

Adults who want to start training strength, return after time away, or stop guessing at the gym, and who want a professional plan to follow.

The program is hybrid: it needs in-person attendance in Zona Río, Tijuana, and has a three-month minimum. If you’re looking for treatment for pain or a current injury, a physical therapy evaluation is a better first step.

Before you enroll

What you can learn first.

  • How the evaluation and the first week work.
  • How weekly reviews and program adjustments work.
  • What the three-month commitment looks like in practice.
  • What happens next if you decide to continue. If not, nothing changes.
