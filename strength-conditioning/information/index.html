Overview

What the program includes.

  • Initial session in person, to define your starting point.
  • Three technique sessions in the clinic during week one.
  • Individual exercise programming, adjusted as you progress.
  • Progress reviews, planned at 30 minutes, at a frequency agreed according to your needs, in person or by video as appropriate.
  • WhatsApp support for program questions.

Who it’s designed for

Built for people who want structure.

Adults who want to start training strength, return after time away, or stop guessing at the gym, and who want a professional plan to follow.

The program is hybrid: it needs in-person attendance in Zona Río, Tijuana, and has a three-month minimum. If you’re looking for treatment for pain or a current injury, a physical therapy evaluation is a better first step.

Before you enroll

What you can learn first.

  • How the initial session and the first week work.
  • How progress reviews and program adjustments work.
  • What the three-month commitment looks like in practice.
  • What happens next if you decide to continue. If not, nothing changes.

Frequently asked questions

What people usually ask.

Does the video call cost anything?

No. It is a 30-minute video call, free, with no obligation to enroll.

Do I need gym experience?

No. Your starting point is defined individually.

Do I have to come to the clinic?

Yes. The program is hybrid: the initial session and the three technique sessions in the first week are in person in Zona Río, Tijuana.

Are the progress reviews physical therapy?

No. They are planned 30-minute reviews, in person or by video, to review and adjust your training; they do not replace physical therapy.

How long is it and what does it cost?

The program lasts three months, with a three-month minimum commitment. Cost and terms are explained in the informational video call, before you decide whether to enroll.
