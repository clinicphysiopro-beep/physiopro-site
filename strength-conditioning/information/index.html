Overview

What the program includes.

  • Three initial instruction sessions, spread over three days during the first week, at our Zona Río clinic in Tijuana, to define your starting point and work on technique.
  • Individual exercise programming, adjusted as you progress.
  • Progress reviews of about 30 minutes and adjustments to your program, on an individualized cadence (weekly, biweekly, monthly or another, depending on your case).
  • General nutrition education: macros, recovery and habits; no meal plan or medical nutrition therapy.
  • WhatsApp support for program questions.

Who it’s designed for

Built for people who want structure.

Adults who want to start training strength, return after time away, or stop guessing at the gym, and who want a professional plan to follow.

The program is hybrid: it needs in-person attendance in Zona Río, Tijuana, and is a minimum of three calendar months. If you’re looking for treatment for pain or a current injury, a physical therapy evaluation is a better first step.

Our approach

Why a physical therapist.

  • Unlike a generic gym routine, your program starts from your own starting point and is adjusted over time.
  • Individual assessment to define your starting point.
  • Appropriate load adjustments throughout the program.
  • Technique and biomechanics applied to every exercise.
  • Education so you build autonomy, rather than depending on a trainer.

Before you enroll

What you can learn first.

  • How the three initial instruction sessions work.
  • How progress tracking and program adjustments work.
  • What the minimum three-calendar-month commitment looks like in practice.
  • What happens next if you decide to continue. If not, nothing changes.

Frequently asked questions

What people usually ask.

Does the initial consultation cost anything?

No. The consultation is free (about 30 minutes, in person or virtual); the program is not free. There is no obligation to enroll.

Do I need gym experience?

No. Your starting point is defined individually.

Do I have to come to the clinic?

Yes. The program is hybrid: three initial instruction sessions, spread over three days, at our Zona Río clinic in Tijuana. The exact clinic address is shared once your appointment is confirmed.

Are progress tracking and adjustments physical therapy?

No. They exist to track and adjust your training; they do not replace physical therapy.

What happens in the initial consultation?

A conversation of about 30 minutes about your goal, your experience and how the program works. It is not a clinical assessment.

How often are progress reviews?

They last about 30 minutes and their frequency is individualized to your case (for example weekly, biweekly or monthly).

Can I reschedule appointments?

Yes, with at least 24 hours’ notice and subject to availability.

How long is it and what does it cost?

The program has a minimum of three calendar months. Cost and terms are explained in the initial consultation, before you decide whether to enroll.
