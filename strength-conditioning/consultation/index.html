The program

One program. Built around you.

A generic routine doesn’t know your starting point or how you move. PhysioPro’s program starts from you: an in-person initial session, technique work in the clinic, and an individual plan with progress reviews and follow-up according to your needs.

How it works

How the personalized program works.

  1. 01

    Initial session

    An in-person session to define your starting point and your goal.

  2. 02

    Three technique sessions

    In the clinic during week one, focused on technique and learning to run your initial program.

  3. 03

    Individual programming

    A plan built for you, adjusted as you progress, with WhatsApp support for program questions.

  4. 04

    Progress reviews

    Planned 30-minute reviews, at a frequency we agree on according to your needs, in person or by video as appropriate, with follow-up between reviews.

The consultation

What the consultation covers.

  • Your goal and training background, in your own words.
  • How the three-month program is structured, including the first week and the progress reviews.
  • Whether the in-person format in Zona Río and the three-month minimum fit your life.
  • Your questions. Program terms are explained here if you want to continue.

Before you message us

The essentials, up front.

  • The consultation is a 30-minute video call. It is free and you are not obliged to enroll.
  • The program starts in person: the initial session and three technique sessions at the clinic in Zona Río during the first week.
  • The program has a three-month minimum commitment.
  • Program terms and cost are explained in the consultation, before you decide whether to enroll.

After your message

What happens next.

  1. 01

    We receive your message

    A member of the team replies on WhatsApp.

  2. 02

    We agree on a time

    We suggest times for the 30-minute video call. Nothing is booked until we confirm one with you.

  3. 03

    We talk

    We answer your questions on the video call. If you decide to continue, we schedule your in-person initial session.
