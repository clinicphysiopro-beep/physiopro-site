The program

One program. Built around you.

A generic routine doesn’t know your starting point or how you move. PhysioPro’s program starts from you: an in-person evaluation, technique work in the clinic, and an individual plan we review and adjust every week.

How it works

How the personalized program works.

  1. 01

    Initial evaluation

    An in-person evaluation to define your starting point and your goal.

  2. 02

    Three technique sessions

    In the clinic during week one, focused on technique and learning to run your initial program.

  3. 03

    Individual programming

    A plan built for you, adjusted weekly as you progress, with WhatsApp support for program questions.

  4. 04

    Weekly in-person review

    Up to 60 minutes each active week to review technique, progression and recovery, plus general nutrition and recovery guidance within our professional scope.

The consultation

What the consultation covers.

  • Your goal and training background, in your own words.
  • How the 90-day program is structured, including the first week and the weekly reviews.
  • Whether the in-person format in Zona Río and the three-month minimum fit your life.
  • Your questions. Program terms are explained here if you want to continue.
