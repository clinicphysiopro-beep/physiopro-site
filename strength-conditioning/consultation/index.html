Your free consultation

What happens in the consultation.

  • About 30 minutes, in person or virtual. Free, with no obligation to enroll.
  • We talk about your goal and your training background, in your own words.
  • We explain how the program works and whether it fits your life.
  • We answer your questions. If you want to continue, we explain the terms right there.

The program

Three months, step by step.

  1. 01

    Week one: three technique sessions

    Three in-person sessions at our Zona Río clinic in Tijuana, spread over three days: you define your starting point and goal, work on your technique and learn to run your initial program.

  2. 02

    Individual programming and tracking

    A plan built for you, with progress tracking and WhatsApp support for program questions. Includes general education on nutrition, macros, recovery and habits.

  3. 03

    Reviews and adjustments

    Reviews of about 30 minutes on a flexible cadence (weekly, biweekly, monthly or another, depending on your case), and adjustments to your program over a minimum of three calendar months.

Common questions

Quick answers.

Do I need experience?

No. We define your starting point individually.

Do I have to enroll after the consultation?

No. The consultation is free and carries no obligation to enroll; the program is not free. Terms and cost are explained in the consultation, before you decide.

Can I reschedule appointments?

Yes, with at least 24 hours’ notice and subject to availability.

Is this physical therapy?

No. It is a general training and education program; it does not replace physical therapy.

What to expect

  • The consultation is informational: a presentation and a conversation about the program. It is not a clinical assessment or a full physical therapy evaluation, it is not a diagnosis or a treatment session, it does not replace physical therapy, and it does not guarantee enrollment.
  • No outcomes are guaranteed.
  • Looking for treatment for pain or a current injury? A physical therapy evaluation is the better first step.
  • Please don’t include medical details in your first WhatsApp message.
