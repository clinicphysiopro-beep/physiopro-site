The program

One program. Built around you.

A generic routine doesn’t know your starting point or how you move. PhysioPro’s program starts from you: three initial instruction sessions, spread over three days, at our Zona Río clinic in Tijuana, and an individual plan with progress tracking and adjustments according to your needs.

How it works

How the personalized program works.

  1. 01

    Three initial instruction sessions

    Spread over three days during the first week, at our Zona Río clinic in Tijuana, to define your starting point and goal, work on technique, and learn to run your initial program.

  2. 02

    Individual programming

    A plan built for you, adjusted as you progress, with WhatsApp support for program questions. Includes general education on nutrition, macros, recovery and habits, with no meal plan or medical nutrition therapy.

  3. 03

    Tracking and adjustments

    Progress reviews of about 30 minutes and adjustments to your program, on an individualized cadence (weekly, biweekly, monthly or another, depending on your case).

The consultation

What the consultation covers.

  • Your goal and training background, in your own words.
  • How the program, a minimum of three calendar months, is structured, including the initial instruction sessions and progress tracking.
  • Whether the initial sessions in Zona Río and the minimum of three calendar months fit your life.
  • Your questions. Program terms are explained here if you want to continue.

Before you message us

The essentials, up front.

  • The consultation lasts about 30 minutes, is in person or virtual, is free, and you are not obliged to enroll.
  • The program starts with three initial instruction sessions, spread over three days, at our Zona Río clinic in Tijuana.
  • The program has a minimum of three calendar months.
  • You can reschedule appointments with at least 24 hours’ notice, subject to availability.
  • The consultation is free; the program is not. Program terms and cost are explained in the consultation, before you decide whether to enroll.

After your message

What happens next.

  1. 01

    We receive your message

    A member of the team replies on WhatsApp.

  2. 02

    We agree on a time

    We suggest times for your consultation (in person or virtual). Nothing is booked until we confirm one with you. The exact clinic address is shared once your appointment is confirmed.

  3. 03

    We talk

    We answer your questions in the consultation. If you decide to continue, we schedule your three initial instruction sessions.
