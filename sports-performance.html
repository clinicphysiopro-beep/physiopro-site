Learn · Sports Performance

Built for capacity, not just recovery.

PhysioPro's performance resources are organized by capacity, not body region: what it takes to return to full performance, build strength and power, run without breaking down, and manage training load. Return to Performance and Jumping & Landing are the first two complete examples.

Performance capacities

What we build back — and build up.

Return to Performance

Criteria-based clearance back to full training and competition — beyond pain-free, to performance-ready.

Strength & Power

Building the force and power output an athlete's sport actually demands.

Speed & Agility

Change-of-direction mechanics and acceleration under real athletic load.

Jumping & Landing

Landing mechanics and reactive strength — the mechanism behind many non-contact injuries.

Running Performance

Structured return to mileage and pace after injury, surgery, or time off.

Conditioning

Work capacity and repeat-effort tolerance for the demands of a full season or competition.

Load Management

Training and competing without exceeding what the tissue can currently tolerate.

Combat Sports S&C

Strength and conditioning built for BJJ, boxing, and MMA — training and competing around injury.
