Medical Disclaimer — the scope and limits of information provided on this website and through WhatsApp communication with PhysioPro.

Información general

1. General Information Only.

The content published on this website — including condition descriptions, exercise guidance, clinical process descriptions, case examples, FAQ answers, and any other written material — is provided for general informational purposes only.

Website content is not a substitute for:

  • A clinical evaluation by a licensed physiotherapist, physician, or other qualified healthcare professional.
  • A diagnosis of any injury, condition, or medical finding.
  • A personalized treatment plan or clinical recommendation.
  • Specialist medical or surgical advice where appropriate.

Every clinical situation is individual. Information that is accurate for one patient may be inappropriate or harmful for another. Do not use website content to self-diagnose, begin treatment, or delay seeking professional evaluation.

Sin relación clínica

2. No Clinical Relationship.

Browsing this website, submitting a contact form, sending a message via WhatsApp, or receiving a general response from PhysioPro does not create a physiotherapist–patient relationship, a clinical duty of care, or any obligation of ongoing monitoring or follow-up.

A clinical relationship begins only when an in-person evaluation has been conducted and both parties have agreed to a treatment arrangement. Until that point, any communication through this website or WhatsApp is pre-consultation inquiry only.

Emergencias

Emergency notice — read before contacting us

If you are experiencing a medical emergency — including but not limited to: chest pain, difficulty breathing, stroke symptoms, acute neurological symptoms, severe uncontrolled pain, post-surgical complications, or any condition that may require immediate medical attention — do not contact PhysioPro. WhatsApp

4. WhatsApp Limitations.

PhysioPro uses WhatsApp as a convenient contact and scheduling channel. The following limitations apply:

  • WhatsApp is not monitored 24 hours a day. Response times vary. Messages submitted outside clinic hours (Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–8:00 PM) will be reviewed the next business day.
  • WhatsApp must not be used for medical emergencies. See Section 3 for emergency contacts.
  • WhatsApp is a third-party platform operated by Meta Platforms. Any personal or health-related information sent via WhatsApp is subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy in addition to PhysioPro's privacy notice. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive personal or health information before a formal clinical intake has been completed.
  • Responses via WhatsApp are general and pre-evaluation. No clinical judgment, diagnosis, or treatment recommendation is implied by a pre-consultation WhatsApp response.

Resultados

5. Results and Outcomes.

Any outcomes described on this website — in case examples, educational content, or condition descriptions — represent individual clinical experiences and are not a guarantee or prediction of results for any other patient.

Clinical outcomes depend on many factors specific to each individual, including but not limited to: the nature, severity, and duration of the condition; the patient's adherence to the rehabilitation program; prior medical history; tissue condition and healing capacity; concurrent medical treatments; and the patient's functional goals.

Timeframe estimates (such as session counts or recovery timelines) are illustrative and based on population-level data. Individual timelines are determined through clinical assessment and reviewed as treatment progresses.

Case studies and examples published on this website have been collected with patient consent. Identifying details may be modified to protect privacy. No case example should be interpreted as a guarantee of similar outcomes in your situation.
