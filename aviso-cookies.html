Política de Cookies

Cookies and Tracking Notice — what tracking technologies this website uses, what data they collect, and how to opt out.

Qué son

1. What Are Cookies.

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website or third-party services to recognize your browser and remember certain information about your visit, such as which pages you viewed or how long you stayed.

In addition to cookies, websites may use similar technologies such as web beacons, pixels, and local storage. This notice covers all of these tracking mechanisms collectively.

This website does not currently use a cookie consent banner. Your continued use of the site after reading this notice constitutes acknowledgment of the tracking described below. If you do not wish to be tracked, use the opt-out options described in Section 4.

Qué usamos

Qué no usamos

3. What We Do Not Use.

PhysioPro does not currently use:

  • Advertising or retargeting cookies (Facebook Pixel, Google Ads remarketing, etc.)
  • Session recording or heatmap tools (Hotjar, FullStory, Clarity, etc.)
  • Affiliate tracking cookies
  • Cross-site profiling or behavioral advertising
  • Persistent login cookies (this site has no user accounts or login system)
  • Functional cookies that store preferences between visits

If any of the above are added in the future, this notice will be updated and the change date will be revised.

Cómo optar por no

4. How to Opt Out.

Google Analytics opt-out
Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. This prevents GA4 from collecting data about your visits in supported browsers.
Browser-level privacy controls
Most modern browsers allow you to block or delete cookies through settings. Blocking all cookies may affect the functionality of other websites you visit. You can also use browser extensions such as uBlock Origin or Privacy Badger to block analytics scripts site-wide.
Do Not Track (DNT)
Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" signal. Google Analytics does not currently honor DNT signals by default. The opt-out methods above are more reliable.
Google advertising opt-out
Visit Google's data and privacy settings to manage how Google uses your data across its services.
General digital advertising opt-outs
Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) and Your Online Choices (EU/UK) provide centralized opt-out tools for participating ad networks.
