Parking

Street parking is available nearby in Zona Rio. The clinic is accessible by car and within close walking distance of public transit routes along Blvd. Sánchez Taboada.

From San Diego

Zona Rio is approximately 30 minutes from the US border. A number of patients commute from San Diego County. Sessions are conducted in English. Payment is in MXN.

Cross-border note: PhysioPro is a Mexican physiotherapy clinic operating under Mexican law. Services are not provided under U.S. licensure. Cross-border patients are responsible for verifying their own health insurance coverage. Insurance coordination with U.S. health plans is not offered. Payment is private-pay only.

Medical Emergencies

PhysioPro is a physiotherapy clinic and is not equipped to handle medical emergencies. If you are experiencing chest pain, difficulty breathing, stroke symptoms, acute neurological symptoms, or any condition requiring immediate medical attention, call 911 immediately. Do not contact PhysioPro for emergencies.
